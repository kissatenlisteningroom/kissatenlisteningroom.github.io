A few things to know.

What works in this preview?

You can explore the setup instructions, help and recovery screens. Your place is saved in this browser. App installation, real invitation redemption and recovery emails are not connected. Payments stay disabled.

An actual app beta will have its own release details, supported devices and access dates. It won’t turn into an automatic charge.

When installing the real app with a free Apple Account, you’ll need to refresh it through AltStore about every seven days. The guide explains this. Keep an existing installation while troubleshooting; deleting it can remove local recordings. Signing in is not a music backup.

What does the website save?

The guide saves your place, computer choice and sample access selections in your browser. Sample invitation codes aren’t sent or saved. Copy buttons copy a setup link; they don’t send it for you.

Website hosting and external pages have their own processing. The app’s account, search and music-provider features can involve online services.

This is a privacy overview draft, not a final privacy policy. Publisher details and hosted-data retention and deletion still need confirmation before an app beta release.

Am I joining the app beta?

Not yet. This test is feedback on the website journey. Real beta invitations, participation terms and release notices are still being prepared. You aren’t accepting an agreement or receiving app access here.

Who reads my feedback?

David reviews reports manually at kissaten@protonmail.com. There is no guaranteed response time.

Original emails and attachments stay in the Proton mailbox. Exported screenshot and diagnostic copies have a separate manual 14-day removal limit; this doesn’t delete original messages or provider backups. You can request earlier removal by email.
